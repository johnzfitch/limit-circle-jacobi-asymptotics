\begin{center}
\estitle{Bohr--Sommerfeld Asymptotics for Limit-Circle Jacobi Matrices}\\[4pt]
\essubtitle{Explicit high-energy eigenvalue laws for every self-adjoint extension of a Jacobi matrix
with links $c\,n^\alpha(1+(\kappa+\kappa'(-1)^n)/n+\cdots)$, $\alpha>1$: offsets that depend on
the extension, rates for balanced links, and the second-order term for shifted powers.}\\[10pt]
{\normalsize John Zachary Fitch}\\[1pt]
{\small\texttt{zack@internetuniverse.org}}\\[1pt]
{\small Version of 22 October 2026}
\end{center}

\esrule

\begin{quote}\small
\textsc{Abstract.} A Jacobi matrix whose off-diagonal entries grow regularly like $c\,n^\alpha$
with $\alpha>1$, without a strong alternation between even and odd entries, is in the limit-circle
case: it has a one-parameter family of self-adjoint extensions $H_t$, each with discrete spectrum.
The growth of the eigenvalue counting function is known up to multiplicative constants, but no
eigenvalue law with an explicit constant was known beyond a few explicitly solvable chains. We
prove such a law, with an explicit offset, for every self-adjoint extension of a zero-diagonal
Jacobi matrix with links $a_n=c\,n^\alpha\bigl(1+(\kappa+\kappa'(-1)^n)/n+O(n^{-1-\delta})\bigr)$ in
the limit-circle range $2|\kappa'|<\alpha-1$. For $\kappa'=0$ the eigenvalues satisfy the
Bohr--Sommerfeld rule
\[
  N_{\lambda_j} I_\alpha-\frac{\kappa\pi}{2\alpha}=j\pi+t+O\bigl(j^{-\min(1,\delta)}\bigr),
  \qquad N_\lambda=(\lambda/2c)^{1/\alpha},\quad
  I_\alpha=\tfrac12\B\bigl(\tfrac{\alpha-1}{2\alpha},\tfrac12\bigr),
\]
with no quarter-period correction in the normalisation of the extensions fixed by the solutions at
$\lambda=0$, and for a shifted power with a curvature term the next coefficient is
$(\kappa_2+(2\alpha-1)/24)\,J_\alpha$. For $\kappa'\neq0$ (dimerised links) the far field is a Bessel
problem of order $\nu=\tfrac12-\kappa'/(\alpha-1)$: measured in units of $\pi$, the offsets of the two
extensions $H_0$ and $H_{\pi/2}$ are shifted by $+\kappa'/(2(\alpha-1))$ and $-\kappa'/(2(\alpha-1))$
from the balanced values $0$ and $\tfrac12$, every other extension follows one of these two laws, and
the remainder is $o(1)$. The proofs combine an exact rotation identity for two-site blocks, an
adiabatic normal form, a Bessel comparison for the far field, and a discrete Olver-type bound for Airy
approximations that we prove from scratch; the last also extends a theorem of Fischer, Burgarth and
Lonigro on singular limits from $\alpha>4/3$ to $\alpha>1$. Applications include the spectra of all
symmetry-preserving self-adjoint extensions of higher-order squeezing operators and the Krein and
Friedrichs spectra of birth--death processes with polynomial rates. For these processes we compute the
critical indices of Berg and Szwarc exactly: $(1-\nu)/2$ for the Friedrichs solution and $(1+\nu)/2$
for every other N-extremal solution, all of them determinate at the critical weight. The numerical
claims are checked against an independent eigenvalue computation.

\medskip\noindent\textsc{MSC 2020.} Primary 47B36; Secondary 34L20, 41A60, 44A60, 47A75, 47B25.

\noindent\textsc{Keywords.} Jacobi matrix, limit-circle case, self-adjoint extensions, eigenvalue
asymptotics, Bohr--Sommerfeld rule, indeterminate moment problem, Airy and Bessel comparison,
critical index.
\end{quote}
